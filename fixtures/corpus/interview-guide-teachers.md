Semi-structured Interview Guide: Early-Career Teachers
======================================================

Study: *Staying or leaving? Early-career teachers' decisions in the
first five years*\
Version 3, approved by the ethics committee on 2 February 2024

Interviewer: read the bold text aloud. Prompts in italics are optional
and should be used only if the participant needs encouragement. Do not
read the notes in square brackets aloud.

## Opening (5 minutes)

**Thank you for agreeing to talk to me. Before we start, I'd like to
check that you've had a chance to read the information sheet, and to
ask whether you have any questions about it.**

[Confirm consent form is signed. Start the recorder only after consent
is confirmed. State the participant ID, date and time.]

Participant ID format: T\-\<school code\>\-\<number\>, e.g. T-07-03.  
Recording file name: same as the ID, plus the date as YYYYMMDD.

## Section A: Background (10 minutes)

1. **Can you tell me a bit about how you came into teaching?**
   - *What else did you consider doing?*
   - *Was there a particular moment when you decided?*
2. **How long have you been at your current school?**
3. **What subjects and year groups do you teach?**

## Section B: Day-to-day work (15 minutes)

4. **Talk me through a typical week.**  
   *What takes up most of your time outside lessons?*
5. **What parts of the job do you enjoy most?**
6. **What parts do you find hardest?**
   - *Is that the same as when you started?*
   - *Who do you turn to when things are difficult?*

[If the participant mentions workload, ask them to estimate hours per
week, including evenings and weekends. Record the number exactly as
given, e.g. "50\*" if they qualify it — the asterisk marks an
estimate.]

## Section C: Support and development (10 minutes)

7. **What support did you get in your first year?**
8. **How useful was it?**
9. **Is there support you would have liked but didn't get?**

## Section D: Future plans (10 minutes)

10. **Where do you see yourself in five years?**
    - *Still teaching? At this school?*
    - *What would make you more likely to stay?*
    - *What would make you more likely to leave?*
11. **If a friend were thinking about going into teaching, what would
    you tell them?**

## Closing (5 minutes)

**Is there anything we haven't covered that you think is important?**

**Thank you very much for your time. As a reminder, you can withdraw
from the study until 31 July 2024 by emailing the research team at
staying.or.leaving@example.ac.uk. After that date the data will have
been anonymised and combined with other interviews.**

[Stop the recorder. Offer the £20 voucher and ask the participant to
sign the receipt.]

* * *

### Notes for interviewers

Keep the tone conversational. It is fine to change the order of
questions if the participant raises a topic early, but make sure all
sections are covered.

Some characters need care when typing up field notes in the shared
template: write 100\% rather than "100 percent", escape a literal
hash at the start of a line as \# so it is not read as a heading, and
write file paths with forward slashes (`interviews/raw/T-07-03.m4a`)
rather than backslashes. A literal backslash must itself be doubled:
C:\\Users\\shared.
