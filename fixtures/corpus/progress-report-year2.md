# Summary {#summary .unnumbered}

The second year of the project has been productive. We completed the
digitisation of the regional broadcast archive, finished the first
round of phonetic annotation, and submitted two papers.[^1] One
planned activity, the community workshops, has been postponed to year
3 for reasons explained in [Risks and changes](#risks).

[^1]: One of these, on vowel mergers in the north-eastern corpus, has
    since been accepted. The other is under review.

## O1. Digitisation {#o1}

All 1,842 reels have been digitised at 96 kHz/24 bit. About 3% were
too damaged for full recovery; for these, we have kept whatever audio
could be extracted and flagged the files in the catalogue.[^damage]

[^damage]: Damage was mostly sticky-shed syndrome on reels from the
    late 1970s. We baked the worst-affected tapes before playback,
    following the archive's conservation guidelines.

    A small number of reels (eleven) could not be played at all, and
    have been sent to a specialist contractor. We expect to hear back
    in November.

## O2. Annotation {#o2}

Annotators have segmented and labelled roughly 60% of the target
material. Inter-annotator agreement on vowel category, measured on a
10% overlap sample, is κ = 0.81. This is in line with what we expected
and with comparable projects.

The annotation guidelines were revised in March to deal with the
large number of overlapping-speech segments in the phone-in programmes.
The revised guidelines are attached as Annex B.

## O3. Metadata {#o3}

The speaker metadata database is complete for all identifiable
speakers (*n* = 3,117). Birth year and place are recorded for 71% of
them. The schema is documented in the project wiki.

# Risks and changes {#risks}

The community workshops (O4) were planned for spring 2024 but have
been moved to spring 2025. The partner organisation that was to host
them lost its venue in January, and the alternatives available at
short notice were not accessible.^[We considered running the workshops
online, but our partner advised that the main audience, older
residents, would be much less likely to take part.]

Spending is slightly under the profile for year 2, mainly because the
workshop budget was not used. We propose to carry it forward.

# Plans for year 3 {#plans}
